FAQ

Questions operators ask us.

Do we need to replace our chargers?

No. Networked chargers connect over OCPP 1.6J or 2.0.1, which most DC chargers sold in India support. Older units get a ₹8,000 sensor kit that does not touch the power electronics.

Which brands do you support?

Any charger that speaks OCPP, including mixed fleets. Operators typically run Exicom, Servotech, Delta and imported units side by side; PulseGuard shows them in one view.

What happens if a prediction is wrong?

A false alarm costs one planned visit. A missed failure leaves you no worse than break-fix today. Accuracy improves as every repair feeds back into the model.

How does the uptime guarantee work?

Chargers that pass an initial health audit are covered by a 98% monthly uptime SLA. If we miss it, part of that month's fee is refunded. Grid outages outside the charger are excluded.

Who owns the data?

You do. We use it only to run the service for you, under a written data agreement, and you can export it at any time.
